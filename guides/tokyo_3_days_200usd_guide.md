# Tokyo: 3 Days on $200/day

Tokyo on a 3 days stay only works if the budget decides the route before the map does. This magazine keeps the walk inside a few neighborhoods and spends $200/day on meals you eat on site.

Spend the cap on one reserved local counter and a single taxi hop, not on a longer checklist. Book a local counter in person or on the shop's own site. Skip chains and hotel dining rooms.

## 3 Days in Tokyo

### Day 1: Kagurazaka

Stay on the slope. Book one counter for the evening and do not cross the city after it. About $200 covers one taxi hop, a reserved counter, and the train.

### Day 2: Ebisu

Use the afternoon for a gallery or a small garden, then eat south of the station. About $200 covers one taxi hop, a reserved counter, and the train.

### Day 3: Daikanyama

Walk the back streets. A taxi is for the hop from Kagurazaka, not for every block. About $200 covers one taxi hop, a reserved counter, and the train.

## Getting Around

Ride Suica or Pasmo on the Yamanote loop and walk the last ten minutes. Changing lines twice costs more time than the fare saves.

Hold the Kagurazaka counter on the restaurant's site, then take one taxi from the station to the slope. Pay the car in the taxi app if you want, and pay the meal at the counter.

## Local Tip

Order at the counter before you look for a seat. On this budget the useful line is the short one inside the market, not the restaurant with a host at the door. Leave when the district fills, even if the list still has one more stop.
