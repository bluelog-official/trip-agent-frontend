# How to Spend 5 Days in Sydney

Sydney's postcard is a harbor that is also a transport system. This 5-day route walks the Opera House and the Rocks, uses a ferry as the trip itself, and gives Bondi or Manly a morning instead of treating every beach as a backdrop for lunch in the CBD.

## 5 Days in Sydney

### Day 1: Circular Quay, the Opera House, and the Rocks

Start at Circular Quay and walk the foreshore to the Opera House. A guided interior tour is worth it if you booked it. The exterior and the steps are still the essential visit. Continue through the Botanic Garden only as far as Mrs Macquarie's Chair if you want the view back, then return. The Rocks is the afternoon: a few streets, a market if the day is right, and dinner there. Do not start the trip in Bondi and commute in. Sleep near the quay, Wynyard, or Town Hall so the ferries are the default.

### Day 2: A ferry that is the destination

Take the Manly ferry in the morning and stay. The ride is the harbor tour. Walk the Manly beachfront, swim if you want, and eat there before you ride back. A faster catamaran saves minutes and loses the point. If the weather is rough, the Watsons Bay ferry and a coastal walk is the substitute, with a hard stop before you promise yourself the whole Bondi-to-Coogee track on the same day. One water neighborhood is enough.

### Day 3: Bondi in the morning, the city in the afternoon

Bondi is a morning swim and a cliff walk as far as you feel like going toward Bronte. Turn around before it becomes a march. Icebergs and the pavilion are the landmarks. Eat in Bondi or back in Surry Hills, not in a restaurant chosen because it faces the Opera House. The afternoon can be the Art Gallery of New South Wales or a simple Darling Harbour loop if you want a second waterfront. Skip a Blue Mountains day trip unless this day is replaced entirely. The mountains are not a side errand.

### Day 4: A bakery, a last quay walk, the airport train

Sydney's last morning should be a bakery and a walk you already know, not a new suburb. The Airport Link train is the straightforward ride from the city stations, and the station access fee is the annoying part, not a reason to invent a cheaper route with two bags. Leave at least two and a half hours before an international departure.

### Day 5: Departure

A beach morning and a midday flight is how people watch the plane leave from a bus.

## Getting Around Sydney

Use an Opal card or a contactless bank card on trains, ferries, light rail, and buses. The ferry is both transport and the view. Trains reach Bondi Junction, and a bus finishes the ride to the sand. Walking is for the quay, the Rocks, and the coastal path. A car is useful in the Blue Mountains and unnecessary inside this five-day plan.

## Local Tip

Book the Manly ferry as a pleasure, not a gap between meetings. Sit outside if the wind allows it, and eat at the destination. A harbor cruise that repeats the same view for ninety minutes is the expensive version of a ticket you could have used to go somewhere and get off.
