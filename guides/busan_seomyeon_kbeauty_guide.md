# Seomyeon K-Beauty: Busan's Practical Shop Street

Busan does not need a Seoul-style beauty pilgrimage if you are already in the city for the coast. Seomyeon is the practical shop street: underground and street-level counters, a subway junction, and enough ordinary pharmacies and multi-brand shops to buy sunscreen without a flagship appointment. Use it as a midday errand between Jagalchi and the beach, not as a full day that cancels Haeundae.

## What Seomyeon is for

### The errand, timed around the trains

Seomyeon Station is the crossroads. Come up, buy what you wrote down, and go back down. A pharmacy with a sunscreen wall and a multi-brand shop next to it cover almost every trip need. Ask for the product in the size you can carry onto a bus to the beach. Staff are used to short visits. You do not owe anyone a skin consultation.

Expect drugstore sunscreen around ₩8,000 to ₩18,000 and a nicer serum, if you actually want one, from ₩20,000 up. Busan humidity is the reason sunscreen matters more than a heavy cream in summer. In winter, add a small hand cream for the sea wind and skip the ten-step set that assumes a dry apartment.

### What not to add

Do not cross the city to a department-store beauty hall after Seomyeon unless a specific shade is sold only there. Centum City can swallow the afternoon. The beach light is the point of Busan. A second mall is how you watch the bridge lights from a taxi window instead of from Gwangalli.

If you are staying in Haeundae, do this errand on the day you are already on Line 1 or Line 2 for the west-side markets. Combining Gamcheon, Jagalchi, and a long beauty consultation is too much. Seomyeon fits because it is a station, not a neighborhood you have to fall in love with.

## Eat, then carry the bag properly

A milmyeon or a pork soup near the station is the Busan lunch, about ₩9,000 to ₩13,000. Eat it before you shop so the bottles are not on the restaurant floor. Then put liquids in a pouch you can pull at a later bus or train. The subway back toward the beach is crowded in the late afternoon. A swinging paper shop bag is how a cushion compact cracks.

## Local tip

Buy the sunscreen you will use on the boat or the beach that same day, and open it at the hotel before you need it. A sealed bottle you cannot find in a tote is the same as not having one when the sea wind starts. Seomyeon will still be there tomorrow if you forgot a lip balm. It does not need a second dedicated afternoon.
