# Seoul: 3 Days of K-Beauty and K-Food

Three days in Seoul is enough for one beauty loop and two meals that are not on a hotel list. This route keeps the shopping in Myeongdong and Seongsu, then spends the evenings on old counters: a market stall, a soup shop, and one newer Korean room that still cooks to order.

Book the skin clinic only if you already have a consultation slot. A walk-in facial at a shop counter is the realistic add-on. Do not stack a clinic, a hair salon, and a palace on the same morning.

## Where to eat

### Day 1: Gwangjang Market

Eat at the market, not next to it. Order bindaetteok and one noodle bowl from a stall with a queue of office workers. Sit down. The meal is the stop.

### Day 2: A Jongno soup counter

After the beauty shops, take the subway back north and eat seolleongtang or a dumpling soup near Jongno 3-ga. Skip the English-menu barbecue chains on the main road.

### Day 3: A Seongsu lunch, then a small Korean room

Seongsu cafes are for the walk, not for dinner. Eat a proper meal nearby, then one reserved counter if the day still has room in the budget. About $100 a day covers the market, the subway, a clinic copay or a shop facial, and one sit-down dinner.

## K-Beauty route

### Day 1 afternoon: Myeongdong counters

Use Olive Young as a supply stop, not the whole afternoon. Pick sunscreen and one lip or cushion shade you can test on your hand. The basement and the side streets have smaller shops with testers that are actually open.

### Day 2 morning: a skin clinic window

If a dermatology clinic near Gangnam or Cheongdam published same-week slots, keep that morning empty. Bring the name of the treatment in Korean on your phone. Without a slot, replace the clinic with a hair consultation in Hongdae and do not pretend a department-store makeup counter is a clinic.

### Day 3: Seongsu warehouses

The converted warehouses south of Seoul Forest are the makeup and pop-up strip. Walk one side of Yeonmujang-gil, buy less than you can carry on the AREX, and leave before the cafe queue becomes the activity.

## Getting around

T-money on the subway is the whole plan. A taxi is for the ride back from Seongsu with shopping bags, not for every hop. Myeongdong to Jongno is one transfer, not a cab.
