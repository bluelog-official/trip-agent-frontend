# Myeongdong Beauty Counters: Buy the Bottle, Skip the Bundle

Myeongdong is the dense beauty street, and density is the trap. Every door offers a set, a member price, and a gift with purchase that is mostly samples you will throw away at the hotel. The useful visit is a short list written before you arrive: one cleanser, one sunscreen, and a lip or cushion shade you have already worn once at a counter. If it is not on the list, it stays on the shelf.

## How to walk the street

### A route with an exit

Enter from Myeongdong Station and pick two multi-brand shops plus one brand counter. That is the whole loop. Start at the shop with a tax-refund desk you can see from the entrance if you are spending enough for the refund to matter. For a single sunscreen, the paperwork is not worth the queue. Pay, keep the receipt in the bag, and move.

Street calls from staff are an invitation to a bundle, not a personal diagnosis. A polite no is enough. If you do go in, ask for the product on your list by name. Let them show you that one item. A skin test on the wrist is fair. A full face of makeup you did not request is how you lose forty minutes and buy a cushion in the wrong shade under their lights.

### Prices you should expect to compare

A popular sunscreen is often ₩12,000 to ₩25,000 depending on size. A cushion compact is higher, and the refill is the number that matters if you already own the case. Write both prices down. Shops on the same street are not identical. The second shop is your comparison, not your second personality. If the prices match, buy at the first counter and leave. Hunting a third discount is how the afternoon becomes a mall.

## Food is separate from the samples

Eat before or after the counters, not with a mask of tester cream still on your hand. A kalguksu or a simple rice set in the side streets off the main pedestrian road is ₩9,000 to ₩13,000 and quieter than the food stalls in the middle of the crowd. The stalls are a snack if you want one. They are not the reason to be in Myeongdong on a beauty errand.

Stop the route when the bags are in your hand. Namsan Tower is a different plan and a different evening. Adding it after three shops means you are climbing with sunscreen bottles. Take the subway back to the hotel, decant anything that might leak, and put the receipts where you can find them at the airport.

## Local tip

Step outside before you pay for a shade. Myeongdong lighting is warm and flattering and wrong. The alley daylight is the color you will see at the airport. If the shade shifts, go back and change it while you are still on the same receipt.
