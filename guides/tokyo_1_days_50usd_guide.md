# Tokyo: 1 Day on Under $50/day

Tokyo on a 1 day stay only works if the budget decides the route before the map does. This magazine keeps the walk inside a few neighborhoods and spends Under $50/day on meals you eat on site.

Choose free grounds, market lunches, and at most one low ticket. Skip tasting menus. Eat at offline counters and market stalls. They do not deliver. Order at the stall and sit nearby.

## 1 Day in Tokyo

### Day 1: Asakusa

Be at Senso-ji before 9:00. Walk Nakamise once, then leave toward the Sumida before the lane becomes a shuffle. Keep transit, tickets, and meals inside $50 for the day.

## Getting Around

Ride Suica or Pasmo on the Yamanote loop and walk the last ten minutes. Changing lines twice costs more time than the fare saves.

## Where to Eat in Tokyo

Eat at offline counters and market stalls. They do not deliver. Order at the stall and sit nearby.

## O2O District

Charge Suica at a station machine or in the transit app, then collect a bento you held at the Tokyo Station basement counter. Ameya-Yokocho stays a cash stall. The phone only holds the station meal.

## Local Tip

Order at the counter before you look for a seat. On this budget the useful line is the short one inside the market, not the restaurant with a host at the door. Leave when the district fills, even if the list still has one more stop.
