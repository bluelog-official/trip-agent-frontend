# Bali Travel Guide: Top Spots & Food

Bali is two trips if you let a scooter and a beach resort split every day. This 7-day route spends the first half in Ubud and the second on one coast, Seminyak or Canggu, instead of adding Uluwatu, the north coast, and a waterfall before lunch.

## One Week (7 Days) in Bali

### Days 1–2: Arrive and stay in Ubud

Use the first two days for this route and leave the second afternoon open.

If you land in Denpasar after midday, do not start a temple circuit. The drive to Ubud is long enough. Use the first afternoon to walk the main ridge of town, find the monkey forest only if you want it, and eat near your guesthouse. Campuhan Ridge is the walk that earns the evening: a paved path above the river, better before the heat breaks. Sleep in Ubud for at least two nights. A resort an hour away looks like a base until every dinner is a transfer.

### Days 3–4: The rice terraces early, then a quiet temple

Keep the next two days inside this area instead of adding another district.

Tegallalang is a working landscape with a busy roadside. Go at opening, pay the local entrance, walk one loop, and leave before the swing platforms become the subject of the visit. Tirta Empul is the better second stop if you want a water temple with a clear ritual order. Dress with a sarong, follow the flow of people, and do not treat the spring as a photo set. The afternoon is for a cafe in Ubud and nothing else. A waterfall added 'because it is nearby' is rarely nearby once the traffic starts.

### Days 5–6: Move to one beach district

Give this part two days so the transfer does not replace the place you came to see.

Check out and go to either Seminyak or Canggu, not both and not also Uluwatu. Seminyak is the simpler restaurant strip. Canggu is more spread out and needs a scooter or a patient driver. Spend the late afternoon on one beach and accept that the water may be rough. A sunset at Tanah Lot is a separate excursion with crowds and a tide clock; skip it if the drive would erase the swim. Eat within walking distance of the hotel.

### Day 7: A slow morning and the airport buffer

Keep the last day slow: the same area in the morning, then the airport buffer.

Bali departures fail when the hotel is in Uluwatu and the flight is at noon. From Seminyak or Canggu, leave at least three hours before an international departure, more on a Friday afternoon. Use the last morning for a warung breakfast and a swim, not a new region. If you still want a temple, Uluwatu at the edge of this day is the wrong add. Save it for a longer trip.

## Getting Around Bali

A private driver for the Ubud-to-coast transfer is the calm choice. A scooter is common and also the most common injury on a short trip. If you ride, use a helmet, skip the night ride, and do not learn clutch technique in Tegallalang traffic. Ride-hailing cars cover Seminyak. Ubud lanes are a negotiation, not a map pin.

## Where to Eat in Bali

Prices are for one person and a normal order, before a drink. Ratings are a traveler's snapshot of consistency, not an official score.

## Local Tip

Order the daily warung plate, not the smoothie-bowl menu, if you want to know what the neighborhood eats. Babi guling and a simple nasi campur at lunch will tell you more about Bali than a beach club at sunset, and they leave the evening free.
