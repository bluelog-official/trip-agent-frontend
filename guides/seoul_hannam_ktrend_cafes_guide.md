# Hannam Cafe Slope: A Trend Afternoon With a Limit

Hannam-dong is a slope of cafes, wine bars, and shops that look quiet until you are standing in a line on a hill. The neighborhood works as an afternoon between Itaewon and the river, not as an all-day pilgrimage. Choose one cafe with a seat, one small shop, and a meal that is food. Then walk down. The view from the slope is the trend. Owning every pastry on the slope is not.

## The afternoon shape

### Cafe first, while there are chairs

Arrive after lunch service and before the dinner reservation crowd, roughly early afternoon on a weekday if you can. Weekend lines at the famous rooms start late morning. If you see a queue down the stairs, that cafe is a photograph, not your seat. The next cafe, with empty chairs and a menu in the window, is the visit. A coffee or tea is ₩6,000 to ₩9,000. A dessert is extra and optional.

Shops here sell objects, clothes, and scent more than souvenirs with a city name. Buy one thing you will use, in the ₩20,000 to ₩50,000 range if you buy at all. A tote you do not need is still a tote you will carry down the hill.

### Where to eat so the cafe is not dinner

Itaewon below the slope has more actual meals than the cafe cluster. A rice plate, a noodle, or a small grill can be ₩13,000 to ₩22,000. Eat it before you climb, or after you are finished, not as a third dessert. Hannam dinners with a wine list are a separate night and a higher bill. This guide stops before that reservation unless you already booked it and know the corkage.

## Getting there without a tour

Hangangjin, Itaewon, and the local buses all touch this pocket. Check which exit is uphill from your cafe so you are not climbing twice. Taxis are common and short. A taxi from Itaewon station to a specific cafe is reasonable when you are tired. A taxi between two cafes on the same slope is how the afternoon gets silly.

Do not combine Hannam with Seongsu and Hongdae in one day. They are three aesthetics and three transits. Hannam is the one that feels residential. Walk quietly, do not film into house windows, and leave the sidewalk clear on the narrow parts. People live above the cafes.

## Local tip

Look at the posted last order. Several rooms close the kitchen or the seats in the late afternoon and become a different business at night. If you wanted a window seat in daylight, that posted time is your deadline, not a suggestion to wait for the lighting to get better.
