Find Local Trip & Food Guides

City routes, neighborhood meals, and guides that passed QA before they go live.

BlueLog Trip publishes city guides that name the neighborhood, the meal, the walk, and the price a traveler actually pays. Each guide is a day-by-day route, not a list of famous photos. The desk checks the place, the timing, and the local cost before a guide goes on the homepage. You can search by city or food, open a single guide, and read the route without creating an account. The map shows cities that already have a published guide. Events explain how a reader report becomes a guide and how points turn into a partner-shop voucher. The wallet keeps that point balance, the report status, and saved guides for a signed-in reader. Shops use the promote page to ask for a partner magazine. The community board collects notes about which guide sentences helped a real trip. This homepage is the catalog of those guides.
